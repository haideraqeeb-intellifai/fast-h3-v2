Method and configuration

Native model generation is 736 × 1280, 362 frames at 24 FPS (15.0833 seconds), matching H3’s spatial and temporal grid. Final exports center-crop eight pixels from each side and retain the first 360 frames with 15 seconds of audio. The final export step is timed separately; original native videos are included.

Each case starts in a fresh process after an idle GPU check; the same prompt, references, seed and graph run once for warm-up and once for measurement. Graph cache is NONE. OS and kernel caches are not cleared. CUDA synchronization and telemetry add profiling overhead. No competing compute process was detected in the recorded one-second samples.
