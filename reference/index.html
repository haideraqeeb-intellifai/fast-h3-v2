Verification & method

Both files passed complete video/audio decoding. Metadata confirms 243 frames at 24 FPS, 736 × 1280, and 32 kHz stereo audio. Each generation executed exactly 4 transformer forwards.

Decoded video hashes match between runs: no. Decoded audio hashes match: yes.

The same seed was used for both runs. Deterministic CUDA algorithms were not enforced; bit-exact video reproducibility is not guaranteed, and any mismatch was not investigated here.

Graph caching was disabled. The warm-up ran first in the same process. OS and kernel caches were not cleared. CUDA synchronization, monitoring, and upstream logging add profiling overhead. Local base checkpoints were not independently content-hashed.

Official loader: 209 bypass adapters and 50 INT8 fc2 merge patches; active bypass hooks confirmed by its runtime canary. The official sampler selected its legacy dual-schedule path for this pinned ComfyUI version.

integrated_multimodal_description: A polished vertical social-media podcast advertisement in a warm modern studio with wooden shelves, soft amber practical lights, two beige lounge chairs, and bright pink broadcast microphones. Keep both hosts' faces, clothing, hair, and the studio layout consistent across all shots.

Shot 1, 0.0-3.5 seconds: Medium close-up of a confident Black woman with long straight black hair, black rectangular glasses, a fitted white top, and blue jeans, seated naturally beside the pink microphone. Shot 2, 3.5-6.5 seconds: Clean cinematic food B-roll, an overhead close-up of black-gloved hands plating two colorful street tacos on brown paper, warm tortillas, grilled filling, purple cabbage, cilantro, lime, and glossy red salsa. Shot 3, 6.5-10.0 seconds: Cut back to the same podcast studio, medium close-up of the second host, a light-skinned brunette woman with shoulder-length wavy hair wearing a charcoal ribbed top, seated in the opposite beige chair. Realistic skin, restrained hand gestures, accurate anatomy, stable microphones, natural blinking and lip synchronization, native-looking UGC pacing. Do not render subtitles, logos, watermarks, signs, or on-screen text.
